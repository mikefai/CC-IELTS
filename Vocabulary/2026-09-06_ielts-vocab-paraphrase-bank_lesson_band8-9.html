Strategy — 3 techniques

1
Context before definition
Use the sentence’s because/but/however — definition follows tone.
2
Trap = familiar but misshapen
Precise beats common — skeptical ≠ enthusiastic.
3
Keep only live meaning
Cross out the part of the word you know doesn’t fit the context.

Practice — timed drill

4. Which choice conforms to Standard English / solves for x?
