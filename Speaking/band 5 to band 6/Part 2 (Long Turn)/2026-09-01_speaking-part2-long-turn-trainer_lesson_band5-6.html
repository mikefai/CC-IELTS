The Long Turn: One Minute of Notes, Two Minutes of Talk

Part 2 is the most predictable part of the Speaking test: you always get a cue card, one minute to prepare, and up to two minutes to speak. Train the format until it feels routine. 3 strategies only.

Today's three strategies

1
Write keywords, never sentences

In one minute you can write about eight words. Nouns and a few verbs — "grandmother · Sunday · kitchen · recipe". Sentences waste time you need for thinking.

2
Use the PPF shape

Say what the situation was like (Past), how it works now (Present), and how you expect it to change (Future). PPF guarantees you keep talking for two minutes.

3
Signpost out loud

Openers and connectors buy thinking time and sound fluent: "I'd like to talk about…", "What made it special was…", "Looking ahead, I suppose…".

Cue-card trainer

Cue Card

Describe a skill you would like to learn.

  • what the skill is
  • how you would learn it
  • how long you think it would take
  • and explain why you want to learn it
1:00

Signposting bank — say these out loud

I'd like to talk about… What made it special was… At the time, I remember… These days it's more like… Looking ahead, I suppose… So all in all…

PPF shape: Past → how it began · Present → the situation now · Future → what you expect or hope. Aim for roughly 40 seconds each.
