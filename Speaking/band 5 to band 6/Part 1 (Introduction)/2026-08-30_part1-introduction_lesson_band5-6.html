IELTS Speaking Part 1: Introduction

Personal Questions & General Topics

Target: Band 5.0 – 6.5
Band 5-6 Focus: Answer questions clearly with relevant details. Use simple past and present tenses correctly. Speak naturally without long pauses. Aim for 30-60 seconds per answer.

Understanding Part 1

Duration: 4-5 minutes

Format: Examiner asks 10-12 questions about yourself, your home, your work/studies, or familiar topics.

Key Skills: Fluency, grammar accuracy, vocabulary range, pronunciation.

Sample Questions & Answers

1. What's your name? ▼
Sample (Band 5-6): My name's Ahmed. I'm from Saudi Arabia, but I've been living in London for about two years for my studies. My friends usually call me by my full name or sometimes just "A" for short.
2. Where are you from? ▼
Sample (Band 5-6): I'm from Riyadh, which is the capital of Saudi Arabia. It's a big, modern city with lots of shopping malls and business centers. The weather is quite hot most of the year, especially in summer. I grew up there with my family.
3. Do you work or study? ▼
Sample (Band 5-6): I'm currently studying Business Management at university. I'm in my second year, so I have quite a lot of coursework and assignments. Apart from that, I do some part-time work in a café on weekends to earn pocket money and gain work experience.
4. What's your favorite type of food? ▼
Sample (Band 5-6): I really enjoy Mediterranean food, especially Italian pasta. The flavors are quite simple but really delicious. I also like cooking at home because it's cheaper and healthier than eating out all the time. My favorite dish is probably pasta carbonara.
5. How do you usually spend your free time? ▼
Sample (Band 5-6): I spend a lot of time with my friends, usually going to the cinema or just hanging out at cafés. I'm also quite into sports, especially football. I play in a university team on Thursday evenings. In the evenings, I like to relax by watching films or reading books online.

Band 5-6 Language Focus

✓ Use these strategies to reach Band 5-6:
  • Simple past & present: "I'm from..." "I've been living..." "I usually go..."
  • Connectors: Use "but," "however," "although," "because" to link ideas
  • Elaborate: Give 2-3 relevant details per answer, not just one-word replies
  • Pronunciation: Speak clearly and at natural speed; pause briefly between thoughts
  • Natural phrases: "kind of," "quite a lot," "I'd say," "to be honest"
⚠ Avoid Band 4-5 errors:
  • Long silences or "um/uh" every few words
  • Repeating the same words: good, nice, interesting
  • Short, disconnected answers: "Yes. I like it. It's nice."
  • Grammatical errors: "I am living here since 2 years"

Practice Tips

Before you practice:

  • Prepare 2-3 sentences for common questions (name, home, work/study)
  • Record yourself and listen for fluency gaps
  • Speak for 30-60 seconds per question—time yourself
