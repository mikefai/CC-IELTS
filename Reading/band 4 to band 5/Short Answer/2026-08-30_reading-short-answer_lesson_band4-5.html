IELTS Reading: Short Answer

Writing Brief Answers to Specific Questions

Target: Band 4.0 – 5.5
Band 4-5 Focus: Find specific information. Write clear, brief answers. Use words from the passage or your own words appropriately.

Understanding Short Answer

Format: You receive questions. Write answers in 1-3 words, NO MORE. You must use only information from the passage.

Challenge: Knowing what to write and how much. The answer must be complete but concise.

Key Skills: Finding specific information, writing concisely, understanding question words (Who? What? Why? Where?)

Critical Rules for Short Answer

  • Word limit: NEVER write more than 3 words (examiners will mark extra words as wrong)
  • Use the passage: Do NOT use general knowledge
  • Capitalization & spelling: Write normally. Proper nouns should be capitalized
  • Answering the question: Make sure your answer directly answers the question asked

Why Students Lose Marks

  • Writing more than 3 words (automatic marks lost)
  • Not finding the exact location in the passage
  • Answering a different question than what was asked
  • Using information not in the passage
  • Spelling mistakes (examiners penalize spelling errors)

Band 4-5 Strategies

Strategy 1: Find the Key Words in the Question ▼
What to do: Identify the QUESTION WORD (Who/What/Where/When/Why/How) and the KEY NOUN. Scan for these in the passage.

Example:
Question: "Where was the university founded?"
Question word: "Where" (location)
Key noun: "university," "founded"
Find in passage: "University X was founded in Tokyo in 1950"
Answer: "Tokyo" (1 word)
Strategy 2: Understand Each Question Type ▼
What to do: Different questions need different answer types:

• What/Which → Often a noun or noun phrase
• Where → Location (place, city, country)
• When → Time (date, year, month)
• Who → Person's name or role
• Why/How → Reason or method (usually needs to copy from passage)

Example:
Q: "When did the war end?" → Answer: "1945" (date)
Q: "Who invented the telephone?" → Answer: "Alexander Graham Bell" (person)
Strategy 3: Copy vs. Paraphrase Wisely ▼
What to do: You can copy from the passage (easiest, safest). Or paraphrase if it makes your answer shorter.

Copy (SAFE):
Passage: "The company was founded in Silicon Valley in 1998."
Q: "Where was the company founded?"
Answer: "Silicon Valley" ✓ (copied, 2 words)

Paraphrase (RISKY at Band 4-5):
Answer: "The Valley" ✗ (unclear, might be marked wrong)

Rule: At Band 4-5, copy when you can. Paraphrase only if it saves you words.

Practice Example

Passage:

The Amazon rainforest covers an area of 5.5 million square kilometers across nine countries, with Brazil containing about 60 percent of it. Scientists estimate that this vast ecosystem is home to approximately 10 percent of all species on Earth. However, deforestation poses a serious threat. Every year, roughly 10,000 hectares of rainforest are lost due to logging, agriculture, and cattle ranching. Environmental organizations are working to protect the remaining forest through conservation programs and indigenous land rights.
Q1: How many square kilometers does the Amazon rainforest cover? ▼
Answer: 5.5 million

Why: The passage states exactly "5.5 million square kilometers." We copy this directly (2 words).

Word count check: "5.5 million" = 2 words ✓ (within limit)
Not: "5.5 million square kilometers" = 4 words ✗ (too long)
Q2: Which country contains the largest portion of the Amazon rainforest? ▼
Answer: Brazil

Why: The passage says "Brazil containing about 60 percent of it" (the rainforest). Brazil is the answer (1 word).

Word count check: "Brazil" = 1 word ✓ (within limit)
Not: "Brazil contains it" = 3 words, but incomplete
Q3: What are three causes of deforestation mentioned? ▼
Answer: logging, agriculture, cattle ranching

Why: The passage lists "logging, agriculture, and cattle ranching." These are the three causes mentioned (3 words, exactly).

Word count check: "logging, agriculture, cattle ranching" = 3 words ✓ (at limit)
Capitalization: Normal lowercase is fine here
Q4: Why is deforestation a serious threat? ▼
Answer: It destroys species habitat

Why: The passage doesn't explicitly explain WHY it's a threat—just that 10% of Earth's species live there (implied reason). This question is tricky. The best answer based on the passage would be to reference that species live there, or that forest is being lost.

Alternative answer: "10,000 hectares lost yearly" (copying the fact about loss)

Note: Questions asking "why" are often hardest because you must find or infer the reason from the passage.

Practice Tips

Step-by-step approach:
  • Read the question carefully
  • Identify the question word and key terms
  • Scan the passage for this information
  • Find the exact sentence(s) with the answer
  • Write your answer in 1-3 words MAXIMUM
  • Check: Does my answer answer the question? Is it clear?
  • Check spelling and word count
Common mistakes to avoid:
  • Writing MORE than 3 words (instant loss of marks)
  • Answering a different question than asked
  • Writing vague answers: "Yes," "maybe," "good" (too unclear)
  • Spelling errors (be careful!)
Time management:
  • Spend 10 minutes on this task (4-6 questions)
  • Read each question (30 seconds)
  • Find and write answer (1-2 minutes per question)

Quick Reference: Question Words

  • What = Noun, idea, thing, event
  • Which = Choose from options (similar to "what")
  • Who = Person's name or title
  • Where = Place, location
  • When = Date, year, time
  • Why = Reason, cause (often needs to infer from passage)
  • How = Method, process (often needs to copy exact method from passage)
