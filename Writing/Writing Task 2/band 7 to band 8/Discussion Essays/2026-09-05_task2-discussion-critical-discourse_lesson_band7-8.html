Strategy — 3 techniques (only 3)

1
Concede to show control
A short concession ('Admittedly, ... yet ...') lifts Task Response to 7 by handling complexity without flipping.
2
Clear position throughout
State your stance in the introduction and keep it consistent. 'Partly' must still lean — 60/40, not 50/50.
3
One idea per paragraph
One claim → explain why → concede a limit if 'partly' → example → link back to thesis. Two ideas = two paragraphs.

Guided example (worked)

Worked — how to apply the 3
Step 1 — Predict: Read Q1 stem, underline what is asked (time? place? number?). Say one possible answer.
Step 2 — Paraphrase hunt: The correct option uses synonyms — “commences at half past five” = 5:30.
Step 3 — Trap check: The distractor is mentioned then corrected (“we planned six — now eight”) — pick the second value.
