## Instructions to student

Write your answer in 40 minutes.
Write at least 250 words.
Do not copy phrases from the question in your introduction.
Spend the first 5 minutes planning and the last 5 minutes checking.

---

## Task

> Some people think that governments should invest more money in public transport,
> while others believe that money should be spent on building more roads.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

---

---

## Structure to follow

| Paragraph | Content | Approx. words |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Introduction | Paraphrase the topic. State your position clearly. | 40–50 |
| 2 — View 1 | The road-building argument, fairly presented. | 70–80 |
| 3 — View 2 | The public transport argument. | 70–80 |
| 4 — Your opinion | Why you side with one view. Do not introduce a brand-new topic. | 50–60 |
| 5 — Conclusion | Restate position. No new ideas. | 25–35 |

## Self-check before you submit

Tick each one honestly. An unticked box is your improvement target.

- [ ] I discussed **both** views. (Discussing only one caps Task Response at Band 5.)

- [ ] I wrote at least four complex sentences (with `although`, `which`, `while`, `because`).
- [ ] I checked every noun: does it need `a`, `an`, `the`, or a plural `-s`?

- [ ] I did not use `In my opinion I think`.
