Quick reminder

The four-paragraph plan

Describe the data in your own words. No opinions, no reasons. About 20 minutes, at least 150 words.

1 · Introduction — one sentence rewriting the question.
2 · Overview — 2 sentences on the main trends or biggest differences. No numbers.
3 · Details — the highest / largest figures, with data.
4 · Details — the rest, grouped sensibly.
Check your overview It should contain no specific numbers, and it should be true of the whole chart — not just one bar or one line.
Question 1

Bar chart

The bar chart below shows the percentage of adults who did regular physical activity in four age groups, in 2005 and 2019. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Figures (%): 18–29: 52 → 61 · 30–44: 45 → 58 · 45–59: 38 → 49 · 60 and over: 22 → 41.

Show a band 6 model answer

The bar chart shows the percentage of adults who did regular physical activity in four age groups, in 2005 and 2019.

Overall, the proportion of adults doing regular exercise rose in every age group over the period. In both years, younger adults were more active than older adults, but the difference between the groups became smaller by 2019.

In 2005, just over half of people aged 18 to 29 exercised regularly, at 52%, and this figure increased to 61% in 2019. The 30 to 44 group followed a similar pattern, rising from 45% to 58%. Among people aged 45 to 59, the figure went up more modestly, from 38% to 49%.

The biggest change was in the oldest group. In 2005, only 22% of adults aged 60 and over did regular physical activity, but this almost doubled to 41% by 2019. As a result, the gap between the youngest and oldest groups fell from 30 percentage points in 2005 to 20 points in 2019.

176 words

Show useful language for this chart
Talking about proportions
the proportion of… / the percentage of… / just over half / almost a quarter / nearly two in five
Small vs large increases
rose modestly / increased slightly · almost doubled / rose sharply / saw the biggest change
Comparing across groups
younger adults were more active than older adults · the gap became smaller / narrowed · followed a similar pattern
Percentage points
fell from 30 percentage points to 20 points (use "percentage points" for the difference between two percentages, not "%")
Question 2

Line graph

The line graph below shows the amount of electricity generated from wind, solar and hydropower in one country between 2000 and 2020, measured in terawatt-hours (TWh). Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Figures (TWh): Hydropower 30 → 35 · Wind 4 → 62 · Solar under 1 → 55.

Show a band 6 model answer

The line graph shows the amount of electricity, in terawatt-hours (TWh), generated from wind, solar and hydropower in one country between 2000 and 2020.

Overall, electricity from all three sources increased over the period, but the growth in wind and solar power was far greater than in hydropower. By 2020, wind had become the largest source, while solar, which started from almost nothing, had risen to second place.

In 2000, hydropower was by far the most important renewable source, producing about 30 TWh. However, it grew only slightly and reached just 35 TWh in 2020. Wind power, in contrast, rose steadily from only 4 TWh in 2000 to 62 TWh in 2020, overtaking hydropower at around 2010.

Solar power showed the most dramatic change. It generated less than 1 TWh in 2000 and stayed very low until about 2010. After that it climbed sharply, reaching 55 TWh by 2020, almost the same amount as wind.

165 words

Show useful language for this graph
Very small starting values
started from almost nothing / less than 1 TWh · remained very low / stayed close to zero
Strong steady growth
rose steadily · climbed sharply · increased dramatically · showed the most dramatic change
One line passing another
overtaking hydropower at around 2010 · rose to second place · became the largest source
Almost equal
almost the same amount as · nearly caught up with · close to the level of
Approximate figures
about 30 TWh · just 35 TWh · at around 2010
