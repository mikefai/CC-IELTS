For the teacher — staging

Timing 50–60 min: Lead-in 6′ → Input 10′ (3 strategies) → Controlled 14′ timed drill → Feedback 10′ → Production 12′.

Staging: Noticing → Doing → Reflecting. Anticipated: list reporting vs selection, repeated ‘and so’, missing hedges.

Strategy — 3 techniques

1
Overview with selection
Name the biggest trend/change without numbers — no overview = Band 5.
2
Cohesion with comparison
while / whereas / by contrast links figures, not sentences.
3
Hedging projections
is expected to / will likely / is forecast to for future years.

Practice — timed drill

Data — Pie Charts: overview first (no numbers), then 2 detail paras with hedged comparisons.

Overview (2 sentences, no numbers): Body 1 — key feature + while/whereas: Body 2 — contrast + hedging:
